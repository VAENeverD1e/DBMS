1.2.3 Artist and Record Label
Artist is a type of User, this role can be achieved by User’s role upgrading. Artist can have
many interactions like: collaborate with other artists, release artworks, receive a report about
their performance in a month, ...
Record label is a company that manages many artists. Artist can work independently or
under the control of one company. Label can assign to the administrator to have strategies for
the platform and artists’ future work.
• Artist: One of the main type of user in the platform, main function is releasing artworks.
Attributes: Genre, VerifiedStatus, TotalFollowers, SMLinks, and ArtistID.
• Record label: Represent record companies, this entity has relation with artist and ad-
ministrator.
Attributes: Name, Country, FoundedYear, ContactEmail and LabelID.
1.2.4 Listener and Play History
Listener is the other main type of user, they have many interactions on the website like:
play music, create playlist, react artist’s artworks, follow artist, ... Each Listener has their own
PlayHistory showing the song that they played.
Listener: Listeners are the core consumers of music content and inherited attribute from
user.
Attributes:FavoriteGenre, Preference, LikedArtworks, and ListenerID.
• PlayHistory: is unique from each user, this entity stores K-nearest song that user have
just listened.
Attributes: PlayDate PlayHistoryID.
1.2.5 Artwork, Single and Album
Artwork is the entity which has the main interaction between listener and artist. Artwork is
the product of the artist, this entity contains single and album.
Album is a type of artwork which includes many single songs. Single is the artwork that the
artist released with only 1 song.
• Artwork The product that is released from artist
Attributes: Gerne, Duration, TotalLike, Title, ReleaseDate, and CoverImage ArtworkID.
• Song: is the artwork with one song
Attributes: TrackNumber SingleID.
• Album: is the artwork with many songs.
Attributes: Total_track AlbumID.
1.2.6 Playlist
Playlist is a weak entity that is created from the listener. Playlist can contain many typical
that is the listener like, they can add many song to many playlist based on the genre, favor or
random.
Attributes: Name and CreateDate.
